The pousada

Everything a few steps from the sand.

Four houses, a restaurant and Maragogi beach right in front. These are the spaces you share with other guests, always with few people and plenty of shade.

01

The beachfront

Wooden tables under thatched parasols, facing the sea. This is where your breakfast basket arrives and the day goes by slowly.

02

Hammocks and swings

Crochet hammocks between the palms and wooden swings facing the sea. The favourite spot to read, nap or do nothing at all.

03

Loungers on the sand

Cushioned loungers, in the sun or the shade, a few steps from Maragogi's warm water.

04

The houses and the garden

Enero Pousada, Enerito, Enero Prime and Enero Deluxe sit among gardens and stone paths that lead straight to the beach.

When the sun goes down

Warm lights among the palms, the moon over the sea and the restaurant open until 8:30 pm.
