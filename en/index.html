    Frequently asked questions

    What are the check-in and check-out times?

    Check-in from 2 pm to 9 pm and check-out from 8 am to 12 pm. Reception is open 8 am to 9 pm.

    Can I bring my pet?

    Yes, we are pet friendly: one pet per suite.

    How does cancellation work?

    Cancel up to 14 days before check-in and your deposit is refunded. After that there are no refunds or date changes.

    Is breakfast included?

    Yes, in the pousada's suites breakfast comes in the picnic basket. Casa Enero does not include breakfast.

    Is there parking and Wi-Fi?

    Yes, both are free.
